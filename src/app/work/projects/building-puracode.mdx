## Overview

Creation of **PuraCode**, a personal tech initiative and micro-studio focused on delivering accessible digital solutions for small businesses, entrepreneurs, and community organizations in the Zona Sur of Costa Rica.

## Key Features

- **Local-Focused Digital Services**: Built practical tools tailored to regional needs, including websites for small businesses, reservation systems for hotels, POS/inventory apps for mini-supers, and QR-based attendance systems for community organizations.
- **Component-Based Development System**: Developed an internal library of reusable UI components and modules using Next.js and Tailwind, enabling rapid creation of CRUD systems, dashboards, and authentication flows.
- **Figma Design Integration**: Created a compact design library in Figma, including color tokens, layout standards, and UI components for forms, cards, navigation, and dashboards—ensuring consistency across all projects.
- **Automation & Data Tools**: Implemented lightweight automation solutions using Google Apps Script, AppSheet, Sheets workflows, and API integrations, allowing local businesses to digitize processes efficiently and affordably.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js**: For building portfolio pages, internal tools, and client-facing apps.
- **Tailwind CSS**: For fast and consistent UI implementation.
- **Figma**: For maintaining brand identity and UI component guidelines.
- **Apps Script & AppSheet**: For workflow automation and low-cost digitalization.
- **Node.js / Express**: For internal APIs and utilities.
- **Supabase / PostgreSQL**: For future modular SaaS projects and scalable data storage.

## Challenges and Learnings

Balancing affordability with scalability presented a core challenge. Building reusable internal tooling—components, templates, and automation modules—proved essential for delivering fast, reliable solutions without sacrificing quality. Additionally, working closely with non-technical clients highlighted the importance of simple interfaces and streamlined workflows. The process also reinforced how automation can provide more value than full software systems in certain local business contexts.

## Outcome

PuraCode now acts as the foundation for a growing collection of development tools, early-stage client projects, and experimental SaaS ideas tailored specifically for Costa Rican small businesses. It has increased development speed, improved project consistency, and created a solid base for evolving the initiative into a scalable digital solutions studio.
