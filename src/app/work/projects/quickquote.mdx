## Overview

This project involved building a clean, professional website for a **New Jersey construction company**, focused on what matters most for local contractors: **trust, clarity, and lead generation**.

The site was designed to load fast, look great on mobile, and guide visitors toward high-intent actions like **calling**, **requesting a quote**, or **booking an on-site estimate**.

## Goal (Business Context)

Construction customers typically make quick decisions based on:
- First impressions (professionalism and credibility)
- Clear services and service areas
- Proof of work (photos and recent projects)
- Easy contact options (especially on mobile)

The goal was to deliver a site that supports that decision-making flow without unnecessary complexity.

## Key Features

- **Mobile-First Layout**: Optimized for customers browsing from phones, with clear navigation and readable sections.
- **Service Pages**: Dedicated sections for core offerings (e.g., remodeling, roofing, concrete, framing—customizable per company).
- **Project Gallery**: Simple visual portfolio to show real work and build confidence.
- **Lead Capture**: Quote request form with validated inputs and clear next steps.
- **Click-to-Call & Contact CTAs**: Prominent buttons to convert high-intent visitors quickly.
- **Local SEO Basics**: Metadata, structured headings, and location-focused content to support search visibility in New Jersey.
- **Performance-Focused Build**: Fast load times, compressed assets, and clean UI.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js**: Static + dynamic rendering for speed and SEO.
- **TypeScript**: Safer development and maintainable code.
- **Tailwind CSS**: Consistent styling with quick iteration.
- **Deployment**: Vercel for reliable hosting and easy updates.

## Challenges & Learnings

The main challenge was balancing a modern look with the practical needs of a trade business. The site needed to feel trustworthy and straightforward, not “over-designed.”  
This reinforced an important lesson: for local services, the best website is one that **reduces friction**—clear info, proof of work, and fast contact.

## Outcome

The result was a solid, lead-ready website that:
- Presents the company professionally
- Makes services easy to understand
- Encourages visitors to call or request an estimate
- Provides a foundation for future improvements (more projects, testimonials, SEO pages)
